1. INTRODUCTION
==========================================================

Welcome to ABC Technologies Private Limited.

This handbook outlines the company's policies, procedures, and expectations. Every employee is expected to understand and comply with these policies.

These policies apply to all permanent, probationary, contract, intern, and temporary employees unless stated otherwise.

The company reserves the right to update policies whenever necessary.
